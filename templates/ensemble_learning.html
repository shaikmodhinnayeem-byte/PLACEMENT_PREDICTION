Ensemble Mechanics

Bagging (RF)
Reduces variance by aggregating de-correlated bootstrap trees in parallel.
Voting
Combines predicted class probabilities across diverse algorithm families.
Stacking
Trains a meta-learner (Logistic Regression) on predictions of heterogeneous base models.

Key Takeaways

Ensemble architectures significantly lower generalisation error, dampen individual model biases, and provide more robust placement predictions for borderline students.
