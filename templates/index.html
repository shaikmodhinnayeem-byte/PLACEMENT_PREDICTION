End-to-End Pipeline Architecture

  1. Data Loading: Verify student records, schema structure, data types, and null indicators.
  2. EDA: Summary distributions, cross-feature correlations, and class balance analysis.
  3. Preprocessing & Min-Max Normalization: Leakage-free train/test split, categorical encoding, and Min-Max $[0, 1]$ normalization.
  4. Linear Regression & Accuracy: Continuous salary prediction evaluated with tolerance intervals (±10%, ±15%, ±20%), MAPE accuracy, and R².
  5. Logistic Regression: Binary classification of placement outcomes with odds ratio analysis and ROC curves.
  6. ID3 Decision Tree: Quinlan's information gain and entropy-based tree with interpretable rule paths.
  7. Boosting Algorithms: Sequential error-correction benchmark comparing AdaBoost and Gradient Boosting (GBM).
  8. Ensemble Learning: Parallel Bagging (Random Forest), Soft Voting, and Stacking meta-models.
  9. K-Means Clustering: Student skill persona segmentation guided by the Elbow Method and Silhouette analysis.
  10. DBSCAN Clustering: Density-based spatial grouping discovering natural cohorts and detecting outlier anomalies.
