ID3 Mathematical Principles

1. Shannon Entropy:

H(S) = - ∑ p_i * log2(p_i)

2. Information Gain:

IG(S, A) = H(S) - ∑ (|S_v| / |S|) * H(S_v)

At each node, ID3 computes Information Gain for every feature and selects the attribute that maximizes entropy reduction.
